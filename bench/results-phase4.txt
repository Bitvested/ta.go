Phase 4 performance rewrites — before/after benchmarks.
Hardware: VirtualApple @ 2.50GHz, GOOS=darwin GOARCH=amd64.
Input data: deterministic-seeded random walk from math/rand.
All benches use -count=3, -benchmem.

#1 movingaverages.Sma — running-sum recurrence
================================================
Before:
BenchmarkSma1k-8     31µs/op    135752 B/op    999 allocs/op
BenchmarkSma10k-8   749µs/op   4497270 B/op   9970 allocs/op
BenchmarkSma100k-8   30ms/op 182946100 B/op  99843 allocs/op

After:
BenchmarkSma1k-8     1.5µs/op    8192 B/op    1 allocs/op
BenchmarkSma10k-8     13µs/op   81920 B/op    1 allocs/op
BenchmarkSma100k-8   154µs/op  802822 B/op    1 allocs/op

Speedup: 21× / 58× / 195× (plan target ≥33× at 100k bars).
Alloc count drops from O(n) to 1 (single output slice).

#2 indicators.Rsi — pre-allocated output, hoisted divisor
=========================================================
Before:
BenchmarkRsi1k-8     6.6µs/op    25208 B/op    12 allocs/op
BenchmarkRsi10k-8     75µs/op   357625 B/op    19 allocs/op
BenchmarkRsi100k-8   935µs/op  4101400 B/op    28 allocs/op

After:
BenchmarkRsi1k-8     4.8µs/op     8192 B/op     1 allocs/op
BenchmarkRsi10k-8     48µs/op    81920 B/op     1 allocs/op
BenchmarkRsi100k-8   520µs/op   802820 B/op     1 allocs/op

Speedup: 1.4× / 1.5× / 1.8×. Allocs O(log n) → 1.
Note: the existing Rsi was already a Wilder recurrence (not slice-
rebuilding), so the plan's ≥10× target was anchored to a worse
baseline than ta.go actually had. The remaining wins are pre-alloc
and a hoisted 1/l in a single delta branch per bar.

#3 bands.Bands — inline running Σx, Σx², single backing slice
==============================================================
Before:
BenchmarkBands1k-8     50µs/op   150289 B/op   1059 allocs/op
BenchmarkBands10k-8   815µs/op  1941595 B/op  10501 allocs/op
BenchmarkBands100k-8   22ms/op 22905704 B/op 104806 allocs/op

After:
BenchmarkBands1k-8     6.7µs/op   49152 B/op    2 allocs/op
BenchmarkBands10k-8     82µs/op  491522 B/op    2 allocs/op
BenchmarkBands100k-8   524µs/op 4800518 B/op    2 allocs/op

Speedup: 7.5× / 10× / 42× (plan target ≥14× at 100k bars).
Two changes: variance via Σx²/l − mean² with running-sum delta
(no per-window stats.Std call) and a single 3·rows backing array
that the [][]float64 result indexes into (one of the two allocs).
